```text

```

1. what real-world operational fact the kind is meant to represent;

3. whether current repository state/actions/events can establish that fact without semantic reinterpretation;
4. whether only explicit external input is defensible at current scope;

6. what exact event/action boundary would be required before a future evidence writer is safe.

## 4. Channel-specific proof obligations

### organizationalContinuity

Define what positive fact distinguishes an operating rebellion organization from a merely high-organization Faction. Determine whether continuity requires conflict-scoped cells/networks/leadership/infrastructure or another typed fact. `Faction.organization > X` is forbidden.

### commandContinuity

Define what proves an operating command structure still exists. Distinguish leadership identity, succession, command connectivity, and generic Faction strategy/influence. Do not infer command continuity from currentStrategy, militaryPower, or an active Conflict flag.

### logisticsAccess

Define what positive fact establishes access to supply, sanctuary, movement infrastructure, financing channel, or other operational logistics. Do not promote Faction.resources, Region scarcity, ContactGraph connectivity, or LandHex ownership into logistics evidence without an explicit semantic contract.

### externalSupport

Define what actor supplies support, what type of support is authoritative, and what provenance is required. `Faction.foreignLinks` alone is a scalar relationship signal and cannot become support evidence. An authored channel does not create a foreign actor, commitment, route, or supply transfer.
