## Mission

Ground why an already-active rebellion can persist, transform, or end when it has no active territorial front edge and/or no faction-controlled LandHex.

The specific late-state blocker to explain is the existing rebellion path that can remain active with `NO_ACTIVE_FRONT_EDGE`. F05_FIX20 must determine the minimum historically and architecturally defensible domain needed to represent non-territorial insurgent persistence and/or nonterminal settlement/suppression without inventing a free territorial writer or treating lack of a front as peace.

This is research + architecture grounding only. Do not implement runtime state, actions, settlement writers, persistence V8, Gate 1F changes, or V02.

## Required research basis

Use primary/high-quality literature on insurgency persistence and civil-war termination. At minimum assess implications from:

- Stathis Kalyvas, *The Logic of Violence in Civil War* — territorial control is important but violence/organization and collaboration are not reducible to conventional front lines.
- James Fearon & David Laitin, "Ethnicity, Insurgency, and Civil War" — insurgency viability depends on conditions enabling small armed groups and state weakness, not only conventional territorial control.
- Barbara Walter, *Committing to Peace* / civil-war settlement commitment problems — negotiated termination needs credible settlement/implementation mechanisms, not automatic peace from battlefield quiet.
- Matanock and related negotiated-settlement/implementation literature — political incorporation and implementation mechanisms are distinct from mere cessation of territorial advance.

Additional relevant insurgency, demobilization, sanctuary, clandestine organization, bargaining, and war-termination sources may be used. Literature is grounding, not permission to invent a generic numeric insurgency score.

## Proof obligations for a minimal domain

If a minimal persistence and/or settlement domain is designable, answer precisely:

### Persistence identity
What conflict-specific fact distinguishes an active non-territorial rebellion from a faction that is merely aggrieved/organized?

### Bootstrap
What creates the initial authoritative persistence state when T018 opens a rebellion? It cannot be inferred from a hidden score or arbitrary label.

### Maintenance/change
Which state-grounded actions/events can strengthen, weaken, transform, or end persistence? Avoid daily countdowns and automatic decay.

### Territory relationship
How does persistence relate to LandHex control? Territory may matter, but `0 LandHex` alone must not equal defeat, and no free LandHex acquisition is allowed.

### Settlement
What authoritative facts distinguish ceasefire/bargaining/settlement/demobilization from mere absence of combat? Settlement cannot be event deletion or direct Conflict removal with no provenance.

### Suppression/defeat
What proves the rebellion can no longer continue? Generic grievance reduction alone must not silently delete an active rebellion unless the domain explicitly establishes operational collapse.

### Player/AI boundary
Which schema-valid actions may player/heuristic/LLM propose, and what authoritative context constrains them? LLM never directly mutates WorldState.

### Replay
What must be logged as ActionRecord/EventStore/runtime state for deterministic replay?

### No pacing cheat
Do not choose a mechanism merely because it closes 1200-day silence.
